# Seance Trademark Policy

**Effective Date:** July 6, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This Trademark Policy governs the use of certain names and branding in
connection with derivative works and products based on the Seance software
project. This policy is supplemental to the MIT License under which the source
code is distributed.

## 2. Scope and Limitations

This policy applies narrowly to prevent consumer confusion in contexts directly
connected to this software project.

**"Noise Factor"** is a distinctive mark associated with this project and its
related tooling. We ask that this term not be used in the names of derivative
products.

## 3. Permitted Uses

You may use "Seance" and "Noise Factor" without prior permission in the
following circumstances:

- Attribution: referring to the Seance project by name in documentation,
  articles, tutorials, or other descriptive contexts.
- Unmodified distribution: distributing unmodified copies of the software,
  provided you comply with the MIT License.
- Academic and research use: referencing the project in academic papers,
  presentations, or research materials.
- Community discussion: discussing the project in forums, social media, or
  community channels.

## 4. Restricted Uses

When creating derivative works or products based on or incorporating this
software, you may not:

- Use "Noise Factor" as the name, or part of the name, of your product,
  service, or derivative work.
- Use "Seance" in a manner that suggests your derivative is the official Seance
  project, or is endorsed by or affiliated with the Seance maintainers.
- Use names, logos, or branding designed to create confusion between your
  derivative and this project.

## 5. Derivative Works

If you create a derivative work based on this software:

- You may not use "Noise Factor" in your product name.
- You must include a notice that your project is not the official Seance
  software and is not endorsed by the project maintainers.

## 6. Requesting Permission

To request permission for uses not covered by this policy, or to ask questions,
please open an issue in the project repository or contact the maintainers
directly.
